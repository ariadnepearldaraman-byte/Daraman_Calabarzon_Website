Things to do

Things to Do in CALABARZON

Make your trip memorable by discovering nature, enjoying local attractions, and learning about Filipino culture.

Outdoor Adventure

Hiking and Mountain Climbing

Explore the mountains of Rizal, Batangas, and other areas while enjoying fresh air, beautiful views, and exciting adventures.

Beach Activities

Swimming and Beach Trips

Visit the beaches of Batangas and Quezon. Swim, enjoy the sea, and relax with family and friends.

Nature Exploration

Nature and Eco-Tours

Discover waterfalls, forests, lakes, and other natural attractions while learning to appreciate and protect the environment.

Culture and History

Historical Site Visits

Visit historical places in Cavite and nearby provinces to learn about Philippine history and the country's heroes.

Local Food

Food Trip

Taste popular local dishes such as Batangas lomi, buko pie from Laguna, and other delicious Filipino foods.

Local Traditions

Festivals and Cultural Events

Experience local festivals, traditional performances, and community celebrations that showcase the culture of CALABARZON.

Travel Tip

Bring water, wear comfortable clothes, follow local rules, and keep tourist spots clean. Always respect nature and local communities.
